# Lasagne alla Bolognese

Serves 6. About 3 hours, most of it unattended simmering. Tastes even better prepared a day ahead and reheated.

## Ragù

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery stalk, all finely diced
- 400 g minced beef
- 200 g minced pork
- 150 ml red wine
- 700 g passata or crushed tomatoes
- 100 ml milk
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt, pepper

1. Sweat the vegetables in the oil for 8 minutes.
2. Add the meat and brown it well.
3. Pour in the wine and let it evaporate.
4. Add tomatoes, milk and bay leaf. Season.
5. Simmer uncovered on low heat for 2 hours, stirring occasionally. It should be thick, not watery.

## Béchamel

- 60 g butter
- 60 g flour
- 800 ml milk
- Salt, nutmeg

1. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, cook for 1 minute.
2. Add the milk gradually while whisking.
3. Simmer until thick like custard. Season with salt and nutmeg.

## Assembly

- 12 lasagne sheets (fresh or no-boil dried)
- 150 g grated Parmesan
- 250 g mozzarella, sliced (optional)

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C.
2. Spread a thin layer of ragù on the bottom of a 30 x 20 cm dish.
3. Layer: pasta, ragù, béchamel, Parmesan. Repeat for four layers.
4. Finish with béchamel, Parmesan and mozzarella on top.
5. Bake 40 to 45 minutes until golden.
6. Rest 15 minutes before cutting.
